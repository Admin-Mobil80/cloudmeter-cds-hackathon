The problem

More cloud accounts, less visibility

It doesn't take seven providers to lose track of spend — five accounts on one provider does it just as easily. Prod, staging, dev, DR: each one is its own console, its own export, its own blind spot.

🧩

The account sprawl blind spot

Whether it's seven providers or five accounts on one, nobody has a single true number without stitching consoles together by hand — and it's stale the moment it's done.

⏱️

The 48-hour telemetry gap

Native billing tools run on batch pipelines. A misconfigured job or a rogue script can burn budget for two full days before anyone sees it.

🏗️

FinOps tools built for FinOps teams

Enterprise cost suites are powerful — and need a specialist and a training budget just to open. Growing teams need clarity, not another platform to learn.
