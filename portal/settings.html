Cost monitoring

How often CloudMeter pulls fresh cost data from your connected cloud accounts

Applies to every connected account across CloudMeter. Cloud providers charge for cost-API requests (AWS Cost Explorer bills about $0.01 per request), so a lower frequency means a smaller monitoring bill. Anomaly detection runs on each refresh, so higher frequencies catch spikes sooner.

Close this CloudMeter account

Permanently deletes this CloudMeter account and everything in it

Closing the account immediately and permanently deletes:

  • All connected cloud accounts and their stored credentials
  • All cost history, breakdowns and anomaly alerts
  • Every team member's access, including your own sign-in

This cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no way for support to recover the data afterwards. Export anything you need first.

Type DELETE exactly to confirm.
