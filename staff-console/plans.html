Plan pricing

What each plan costs. India is billed in INR with GST added; everywhere else is billed in USD with no tax.

Changing a price does not re-price anyone. A running subscription keeps the amount it was created at — gateways bill a fixed figure per cycle — so a new price applies to new checkouts and to anyone who changes plan.

Payment gateway

Who processes the money. India and the rest of the world are decided separately.

India INR + GST

Fixed to Razorpay. It is the only one of the three that settles INR to an Indian entity and supports UPI Autopay mandates, so there is nothing to choose.

Razorpay All Indian customers, always.

Outside India USD, no tax

Switching does not move anyone. A gateway cannot hand a live mandate to a competitor, so subscriptions already running keep billing where they started until they are cancelled and re-created. This setting governs new checkouts only.
