These prices only pre-fill a field. When a customer picks a tool and plan, the cost box starts at the figure below and they can change it — nothing recorded here alters a cost any customer has already entered, and no reported spend is calculated from it. Leave a price empty where there is no single public figure, such as an enterprise contract.

The INR columns are what the vendor charges in India, GST included — the figure on their own Indian pricing page, copied across exactly, never the dollar price converted. It is shown to the customer as the whole price, so type ₹2,399 and ₹2,399 is what they see; nothing is added to it. Leave it empty and an Indian customer is quoted the USD price plus GST rather than a rupee figure nobody is billed.

The per-year columns are the whole amount paid up front, not a monthly equivalent. Most vendors only advertise an annual deal as "$17/month billed annually" and never state the year's total — leave those empty rather than multiplying by twelve, which states a price the vendor doesn't. A customer choosing Yearly then types what they were charged. A plan sold only by the year is one with the per-month boxes left empty.
